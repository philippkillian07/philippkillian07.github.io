Computer Science Graduate from ELTE University

Hey there, I'm Philipp! I just finished my B.Sc. in Computer Science at ELTE in Budapest, after growing up mostly between Germany and Hungary. I like software that touches something real, data that has to stay correct, systems other people depend on, code that runs somewhere other than my own laptop.

I have studied in many places. High school in Portland, Oregon, an exchange semester at POSTECH in South Korea. I am currently a Data Platform Trainee in Budapest. I've also spent a semester teaching functional programming to students, which taught me as much about explaining code as writing it.

Outside of work I enjoy being outdoors. In the summer you can find me camping, and in the winter I am in the alps skiing.
